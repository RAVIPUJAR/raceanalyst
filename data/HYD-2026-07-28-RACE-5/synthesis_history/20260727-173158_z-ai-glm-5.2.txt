FIELD ASSESSMENT
=================

1. EK                    **** STRONG CONTENDER
2. LA QUINTA             **** STRONG CONTENDER

EK is the only horse in the field with a BIG trainer (Magan Singh, 40%) and
BIG jockey (Akshay Kumar, 25%) combination, plus a clear jockey upgrade from
P Sai Kumar. This is the sole genuine intent signal in an otherwise cold
field. Trackwork is the standout of the race (SHARP, -10s 1200m on the eve),
equipment changes are net positive (blinkers/cross noseband/hanging bit
removed), and direct head-to-head form shows EK 2.5L ahead of MY HONEY and
11L ahead of FAIZAH on the same card. Competition form validation is
STRONG UPGRADE (AUTHORITARIAN placed 2nd in higher class; DIVINE FAITH
consistently placed). The caveats are that 1200m is untested (both prior
runs at 1100m), and a recent stumble plus fitness certificate requirement
in July introduces a soundness risk. From gate 2, EK should control the
pace as the sole front-runner.

LA QUINTA is the strongest form horse with a recent WIN and a competitive
2nd at 1200m (beaten just 1.25L). Competition form validation is STRONG
UPGRADE -- beaters RED HOT PUNCH and FOX WORTH both won next start, and
SHARANGA placed in higher class. The only pedigree signal in the field
(dam Xtreme, producer of multiple Cup/Trophy winners) adds depth. Carries
the only sweepstakes nomination (historic G1 Colts Championship Stakes,
WEAK signal). However, connections are ALL SMALL with a lateral jockey
booking, trackwork is thin (just an easy 800m spin), hind bandages and a
March veterinary withdrawal add medical risk. From gate 4, a stalking trip
suits the compressed-pace scenario.
