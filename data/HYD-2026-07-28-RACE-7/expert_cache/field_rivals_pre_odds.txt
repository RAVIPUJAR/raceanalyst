**A) Likely pace scenario**  
There is no committed 1400m front-runner in the field. CORINTHIAN can lead or press (but draws 12 and has a prior SLOWLY AWAY), while TORCHBEARER and ARMSTRONG may take minor forward positions. The bulk of the field—WOLF CREEK, BLUE PANTHERESS, CLEFAIRY, CLARA, CHINA TOWN—want to settle midfield or worse. With 16 runners and no confirmed rope-puller, the tempo will likely be **honest but not scorching**, bunching into the final bend. That suits stalkers with a change of gear (WOLF CREEK, N R I RANGE, EMPERORS CHARM, BLUE PANTHERESS) more than deep closers who need a furious pace to close into (BLACK DUST, PERFECT LIGHT, CLARA). Horses from outside gates (13–16) will either burn energy pushing forward or concede ground on the bends.

Tractability is key because the early speed will be patchy. Low-to-mid handicap weight and a clean departure from the gates are important in this size field.
