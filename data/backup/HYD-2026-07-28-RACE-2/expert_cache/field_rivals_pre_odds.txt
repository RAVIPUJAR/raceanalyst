# FIELD ASSESSMENT

**A) Likely pace scenario**  
With **NORTHERN SKY** (Gate 1) the only confirmed forward runner and a dozen others wanting to settle midfield or deeper, expect a **moderate, compressing tempo** early. ADVAIT (Gate 8) should sit 3rd–5th outside the leader. The deep closers—MASTER CLASS (11), KINSHIP (6), TWILIGHT KING (7), WESEETHESAMESUN (13), WIND CASTLE (9)—will be dropping 6–10 lengths off the pace. Because the tempo is unlikely to be brutal, the deep closers will need real traffic luck to weave through a bunched straight. The profile that wins is a **tractable stalker with late dash** rather than a pure back-marker.

**B) Winner profile that fits today**  
A 3yo with **prior Hyderabad 1200m form**, gate versatility (able to sit 3rd–5th), and a sustained sectional in the low-24s or faster late. Tractability is important because the straight will be crowded.

**C) Field ranking — top 5**

1. **ADVAIT** — The most proven 1200m performer in the race. He confirmed superiority over the majority of this deep-closing bunch on 24-Mar (2nd, beaten only a length) and his sectionals sit right on the benchmark. From Gate 8 he can settle handily and get first run on the deep closers.  
2. **NORTHERN SKY** — Drawn ideally in Gate 1 and owns the **best sectionals in the race** (23.58 last 400m). His 1600m–1400m form suggests he will relish the drop to a strongly-run 1200m. The only caveat is he is unproven at the trip, but the way he finishes suggests he will be roaring home.  
3. **MASTER CLASS** — Solid 2nd-over-this-C&D form and a validated competition line (beaten by a subsequent handicap winner). However the **wide Gate 11**, greenness, and confirmed slow-start tendency mean he will give the principals a start. Classy but vulnerable to traffic.  
4. **ANEMARA** — Debuted 4th at 1100m with genuine late headway. Unexposed 3yo who should strip fitter for the 1200m step. Draw 10 is awkward but not fatal in a moderate pace.  
5. **TWILIGHT KING** — Has the raw sectional speed (23.78) but is exposed as a 6th-place horse twice. He can sweep into a minor cheque if the leaders implode and he stays straight.

**D) Danger horses outside top 5**  
**LOVE ALL** (Gate 2) and **VELVERA** (Gate 3) are complete mysteries. If either is a precocious maiden with tactical speed from an inside draw, they could sit nearer the lead than the market expects and pinch a place. No data supports a stronger case.

**E) Horses to oppose (and why)**

- **KINSHIP** — Shied and drifted at the start, carries a vet-grounds flag, and was beaten 16¾ lengths by Advait in a race where he had every chance. No corrective evidence since.  
- **WESEETHESAMESUN** — Ran erratic early and finished last of 10 in the Rising Star Plate. Sectionals flattered by detached clear air. Likely non-competitive again.
