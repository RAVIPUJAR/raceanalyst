ONCE YOU GO BLACK is the only horse in the field with recent Group

ONCE YOU GO BLACK holds the edge on: weight-adjusted rating (124 vs

vs VOLATILE), and day-results 1600m read (AT 109 vs BELOW 116). Both
carry MODERATE sweepstakes signals with TARGET 1600m nominations.

Key dangers outside the top 2: ALFONSINE (STRONG sweepstakes, 4yo

RACE TEMPO AND TACTICAL NOTES

There is no dedicated front-runner in the field. ALFONSINE and
XANTHE are the most likely to sit within 3-4 lengths of the lead
from their inside draws. MAYNE MAGIC, PROMISEOFTHEFUTURE and
GALAHAD will drop out the back half-dozen. With rails out 5.5m,
the inside 1-3 path is advantageous; drawn outside 6, ALFONSINE,
PROMISEOFTHEFUTURE and SHAMROCK risk three-wide runs. Pace
pressure will be honest rather than fierce. A muddling tempo
would slightly blunt the deep closers (MAYNE MAGIC, POTF) and
favour stalkers (XANTHE, ALFONSINE, ONCE YOU GO BLACK).

The firm surface (penetrometer 3.2cm, firm side of good) ampli-
fies the importance of soundness. Horses with chronic soft-tissue
histories (SHAMROCK, PROMISEOFTHEFUTURE, MAYNE MAGIC, GALAHAD)
face increased stress through the back straight on the thin
cushion.

SUMMARY

The Karnataka Mile Championship Cup shapes as a contest between
ONCE YOU GO BLACK's peaking profile and MAYNE MAGIC's class
resilience. ONCE YOU GO BLACK brings the only recent Group 1600m
form, a perfect connections trio, a READY TO STRIKE body weight
signal, and an engineered ratings drop. MAYNE MAGIC brings the
best historical mile record in the field and proven sectional
speed but enters on an unverified swim-managed prep with a slow
1600m clock hanging over his rating.

The StrAtegic edge sits with ONCE YOU GO BLACK on weight-adjusted
ratings, trackwork recency, connections intent, and body weight
signal. MAYNE MAGIC is the clear danger if his historical class
overrides the current prep concerns. ALFONSINE and XANTHE are
live place prospects with STRONG sweepstakes signals but each
carries a disqualifying flaw (cold T+J/overweight for ALFONSINE;
low rating/second string for XANTHE).
