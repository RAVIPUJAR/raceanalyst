TRACK CONDITIONS ASSESSMENT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

 Going quality:
   The official line reads good, but the penetrometer reading sits
   firmly in the firm band. The surface will play quick — fastest
   from the front, the bias note flags it as a speed/front-runner
   friendly setup. Cushion is thin on top, which makes the closing
   stages more taxing for stamina horses and increases the jar on
   joints and tendon structures through the back straight.

 False rails impact:
   The rails are displaced by about five-and-a-half metres outward
   from their original position for the entire 1600 metres — from
   the mile start all the way to the winning post. This means the
   racing line is wider than the conventional strip, and the field
   travels a marginally longer distance around the turn. Inside
   draws are mildly disadvantaged because the field has to negotiate
   the turn on a surface further out, while prominent racers drawn
   anywhere from low to middle can hold their line without burning
   extra energy crossing. Wide draws lose less than they would on a
   standard strip because the rails have been pushed out.

 Surface-specific caution:
   Firm going amplifies the importance of soundness. Horses with
   chronic soft-tissue histories in the medical file (SHAMROCK,
   MAYNE MAGIC, PROMISEOFTHEFUTURE prominent, GALAHAD to a lesser
   extent) need their trackwork sharpness weighed against the extra
   stress on the surface at the business end of the race. Trackwork
   SHARP times on a fast morning strip will translate less cleanly
   to a firm race-day surface than to a good one.
